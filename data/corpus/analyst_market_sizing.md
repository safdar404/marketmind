# Meridian Research — AI Customer Service Software: Market Sizing 2026

## Market size
Meridian estimates global spending on AI-enabled customer service software at $4.1 billion in 2025, up from $2.9 billion in 2023. The estimate covers licence and usage fees for help-desk, chatbot and agent-assist products, and excludes contact-centre telephony and services.

## Outlook
Meridian projects the market to reach $7.0 billion in 2028 under its base-case scenario, with a range of $5.8 billion to $8.1 billion.

## Method
The estimate is built bottom-up from vendor disclosures and 140 buyer interviews.

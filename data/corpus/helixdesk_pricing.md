# HelixDesk Pricing

## Plans
Starter costs $59 per agent per month, billed annually, and includes email and chat channels with agent assist.
Growth costs $99 per agent per month, billed annually, and adds Helix Resolve autonomous resolution with 1,000 resolutions per month included.
Enterprise pricing is available on request from the HelixDesk sales team.

## Free trial
HelixDesk offers a 14-day free trial. There is no permanent free plan.

# HelixDesk AI — Product Overview

## Positioning
HelixDesk positions itself as an AI-first service platform for enterprise and upper mid-market support organisations with more than 200 agents. Its flagship assistant, Helix Resolve, drafts replies, summarises tickets and resolves routine requests autonomously.

## AI capabilities
Helix Resolve offers autonomous resolution of password, billing and order-status requests, real-time agent assist with suggested replies, automatic ticket summarisation, and sentiment-based routing. HelixDesk states that Helix Resolve is grounded on the customer's own help-centre content and cites the article used.

## Integrations
HelixDesk lists more than 120 native integrations, including Salesforce, SAP, Shopify, Slack and Microsoft Teams.

## Deployment
HelixDesk is delivered as multi-tenant cloud software. A single-tenant private cloud option is available on the Enterprise plan.

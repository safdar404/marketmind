# Orbit CX — Enterprise Customer Experience Cloud

## Positioning
Orbit CX sells a customer-experience suite to large enterprises in telecoms, banking and utilities, typically with more than 1,000 agents.

## AI capabilities
Orbit CX Insight provides agent assist, quality scoring of every conversation, and workforce forecasting.

## Deployment
Orbit CX is available in public cloud and in dedicated regional instances for regulated industries.

## Commercial terms
Orbit CX pricing is negotiated individually and is not published.

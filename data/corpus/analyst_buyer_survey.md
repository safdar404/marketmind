# Meridian Research — Buyer Priorities in Support Software 2026

## Key findings
In a survey of 310 support leaders, 61% of mid-market buyers ranked integration with their CRM as the top selection criterion. 47% said they would consider pricing based on resolved conversations instead of per-seat licences.

## Concerns
54% of respondents cited accuracy of AI answers as their main concern, and 38% cited data-protection requirements.

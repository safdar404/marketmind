# TicketPilot — Simple Help Desk for Small Teams

## Positioning
TicketPilot is built for startups and small businesses with fewer than 25 agents.

## AI capabilities
TicketPilot Copilot drafts replies and tags tickets automatically. It does not offer autonomous resolution.

## Data residency
Customer data is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt) or the United States (Virginia), selected at sign-up.

## Pricing
TicketPilot is free for up to 3 agents. The Team plan costs $19 per agent per month and the Business plan costs $39 per agent per month.

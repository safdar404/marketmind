# TechLedger — Spending on AI support tools surges

## Headline figure
Companies spent $6.8 billion on AI customer support software in 2025, according to figures compiled by TechLedger from industry sources.

## Drivers
Executives interviewed by TechLedger said that autonomous resolution and multilingual chat were the main reasons for new purchases.

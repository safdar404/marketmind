# Nimbly Assist — Pay Only for Resolutions

## Positioning
Nimbly Assist is an AI agent that sits in front of an existing help desk and resolves conversations before they reach a human. It targets e-commerce brands.

## Pricing
Nimbly Assist does not charge per seat. Customers pay $0.90 per conversation that is resolved without human handoff. There is no free tier; a 30-day pilot is available.

## Integrations
Nimbly Assist connects to Shopify, Gorgias, HelixDesk and Converso.

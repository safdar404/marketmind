# Converso Pricing

## Plans
Free: up to 2 agents with a shared inbox and basic chatbot.
Essentials costs $29 per agent per month.
Pro costs $69 per agent per month and requires a minimum of 10 seats.
Enterprise is priced at $119 per agent per month with a minimum of 50 seats.

## Billing
All paid plans are billed annually; monthly billing adds 20%.

# SupportStack Reviews — Converso user reviews summary

## Summary
Converso averages 4.4 out of 5 across 812 verified reviews. Reviewers praise setup speed and the multilingual chatbot.

## Common complaints
Several mid-market reviewers report that the Pro plan required a minimum of 25 seats when they signed in early 2026, which made the plan expensive for smaller teams. Reporting dashboards are described as basic.

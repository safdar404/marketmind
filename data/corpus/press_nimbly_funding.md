# Nimbly Assist raises $18 million Series A

## Announcement
Nimbly Assist has raised an $18 million Series A round to expand its outcome-priced AI support agent. The company says it now serves around 400 online retailers.

## Context
Investors cited the shift from per-seat licences toward pricing tied to resolved conversations as a reason for the round.

# TechLedger — What the EU AI Act means for support chatbots

## Transparency
Under the EU AI Act, customers must be told when they are interacting with an AI system. Support chatbots deployed in the EU therefore need clear disclosure.

## Implications
Legal experts quoted by TechLedger expect vendors to add disclosure banners and audit logs to their chat widgets.

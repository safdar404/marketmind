# Meridian Research — Pricing Models in AI Support

## Shift to outcome-based pricing
Meridian observes that vendors increasingly combine per-seat licences with charges per AI resolution. Of the 12 vendors tracked, 7 now publish a per-resolution price.

## SMB pricing
Entry tiers for small teams cluster between $19 and $35 per agent per month among the vendors Meridian tracks.

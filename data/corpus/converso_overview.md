# Converso — Conversational Support for Growing Teams

## Positioning
Converso targets small and mid-sized businesses with 5 to 150 support agents who want a shared inbox with AI built in.

## AI capabilities
Converso AI suggests replies, translates conversations in 38 languages, and deflects common questions through a website chatbot trained on the help centre. Converso does not currently offer fully autonomous ticket resolution for billing actions.

## Integrations
Converso integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, HubSpot, Slack and WhatsApp Business.

# TechLedger — HelixDesk acquires chatbot startup Parla

## Deal
HelixDesk has acquired Parla, a 30-person conversational AI startup, for an undisclosed sum. It is HelixDesk's second acquisition in twelve months.

## Market context
TechLedger notes that larger help-desk vendors have been buying smaller AI specialists, but no analyst figures on deal volume were cited.
